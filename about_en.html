CV

Yin Yi is a musician, artist, and curator born in Shanghai. His creative practice revolves around "auditory-sound," spanning multimedia domains such as sound art, music, installation, performance, video, text, and theater.

Yin formed the Dead Poets Society in 1998, a slowcore band in which he was the lead singer and also a bassist. Yin turned to digital music three years later and he became a founding member of the avant-garde performance collective Zuhe Niao as a musician in 2005. His musical creation started to cover a wide range, e.g., live music performance, contemporary dance, and physical theatre. Commissioned by individual artists and modern dance groups, Yin's works are frequently heard in the performances of Zuheniao (Shanghai), Tanzcompagnie Rubato (Berlin), Guangdong Modern Dance Company, Xie Xin Dance Company, Ergao Performance etc. In recent years, Yin starts to focus on live laptop performances and site-specific soundscape compositions rooted in field recordings.

In the creation of sound art, Yin Yi is known for his philosophical and poetic ideas. His observation and reflection on public space is both acute and deep, and his works are both avant-garde and critical. He also proposes the notion of "auditory – sound space" and attempts to establish a working method around this concept, bringing his praxis to the discourse of multi-disciplinary collaborations.

Yin Yi's work has been featured in numerous art festivals and institutions, including RAM Assembles（2023），Echigo-Tsumari Art Triennial (2018), the 11th Shanghai Bienniale (2016) and Sound Art China (2013), Harvest Work (New York), Power Station of Art (Shanghai), Yuz Museum，Rock Bund Museum(RAM), Unsafe and Sound Festival (Vienna), HongKong Art Centre (HongKong), Museum of Contemporary Art (Shanghai), Ming Contemporary Art Museum (Shanghai), Shanghai International Dance Centre, OCAT Shenzhen, Pingshan Museum (Shenzhen), Shanghai Urban Space Art Season, etc.

Since 2011, Yin Yi has been paying close attention to the artistic ecology of experimental music and sound art across China. Activities that he recently curated include, "Formant" (a sound art exhibition), "Co-Sound" (sound performance festival), "Sonic Transmission" (exchange project between Shanghai and HongKong), etc. In 2012 Yin and choreographer Liu Yanan founded artist running alternative art space - BM Space. Since 2013, she has collaborated with art institutions to curate exhibitions and programs, including the Rockbund Art Museum, OCAT Shanghai, Shanghai Duolun Museum of Modern Art, and West Bund Museum.
