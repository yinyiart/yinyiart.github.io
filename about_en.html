Yin Yi Musician Artist Curator Texts CV CN

Yin Yi is a musician, Artist, curator. Based in Shanghai. Establishing "Auditory-Sound" as the core of his practice, Yin Yi regards it as a fundamental mode of perception, thinking, and being. His work spans sound art, music, installation, performance, video, text, and theater.

His musical career began in a Slowcore band as a bassist and vocalist, later shifting to digital composition and Laptop Performance. In 2005, he co-founded the artist collective Zuhe Niao. His early practice encompassed live music performance, contemporary dance, and physical theater; recent works focus on hybrid ensembles combining Field Recording with acoustic instruments.

Extending from music into sound art, his practice is permeated by a critical reflection on the history of music and sound art. Focusing on the urban context —specifically Shanghai— he delves into the logic and mechanisms of sound operation in public spaces. Simultaneously, through a unique lens of philosophical reflection and poetic sensibility, he reveals the social mechanisms and micropolitical power relations underlying sound. Building upon these inquiries, he proposes the concept of "Auditory-Sound Space," developing corresponding methodologies to facilitate deep interdisciplinary collaborations.

His works have been presented at RAM Assembles (2023), Shenzhen-Hong Kong Bi-City Biennale of Urbanism/Architecture (2022), Echigo-Tsumari Art Triennale (2018), Shanghai Biennale (2016), Sound Art China (2013), Unsafe and Sound Festival (2013), Frischzelle Festival (Cologne & Düsseldorf, 2012), and Shanghai Urban Space Art Season (2019), among others. Exhibiting institutions include: Yuz Museum, Power Station of Art (PSA), Rockbund Art Museum, West Bund Museum, OCAT Shenzhen, UCCA Edge, Shanghai Duolun Museum of Modern Art, Shanghai International Dance Center, Chengdu Art Museum, Museum of Sound Art (Beijing), Chengdu Academy of Fine Arts Museum, MCA Shanghai, MoCA Shanghai, Pingshan Art Museum (Shenzhen), Harvest Work (New York), and Hong Kong Cultural Centre.

Since 2011, Yin Yi has been dedicated to the ecology of avant-garde music and sound art, curating the "Formant" Sound Art Exhibition, the "Sound Transmission" exchange project, and the "Co-sound" Experimental Music Festival. In 2012, he co-founded BM Space with choreographer Liu Yanan, an artist-run space dedicated to avant-garde music and contemporary dance. Since 2013, he has been invited to collaborate with major institutions including the Rockbund Art Museum, OCAT Shanghai, Shanghai Duolun Museum of Modern Art, and West Bund Museum on various exhibitions and programs.
