Yin Yi Musician Artist Curator Writing About CN

Yin Yi's musical career began with the slowcore band, serving as bassist and lead vocalist.

In 2000, he turned to digital music composition and laptop performance. In 2005, as a musician, he became a founding member of the avant-garde performance group "Zuheniao" (Shanghai).

Yin Yi's early musical creations include: live music performance, contemporary dance, and physical theatre. His music has been commissioned by performance groups including "Zuhe Niao" (Shanghai), Rubato Dance Company (Berlin), Guangdong Modern Dance Company, Xie Xin Dance Theatre, and Ergao Performance.

In recent years, his musical work has focused on hybrid music based on field recordings and acoustic instruments.
